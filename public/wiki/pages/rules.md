# Rules & Code of Conduct

## Community

1. Treat fellow OMDB users with respect.
2. Avoid actions that disrespect the site's intended functionality (such as manipulating votes in a group, or using multiaccounts).
3. Do not encourage other users who are engaging in rule-breaking behaviour.

Violation of these rules may lead to permanent account suspension.

---

## User-Generated Content

User-Generated Content refers to comments, reviews, and lists.

1. Please only use English for user-generated content to ensure everyone can understand and participate.
2. Do not post insensitive content. This includes racism, sexism, homophobia and transphobia.
3. Engage in discussions with respect and courtesy. Disagreements are fine, but avoid personal attacks and insults.
4. Remember that tastes vary. Be open to different viewpoints and diverse preferences.
5. Derailing ongoing conversations are not allowed. Do not be disruptive.
6. Do not spam, advertise, or repeatedly post unsolicited content. This includes excessive self-promotion, referral links, and promotional material.

Violation of these rules may result in your content being removed. Repeated offenses can lead to permanent account suspension.

---

Moderators may take action against behaviour that is harmful to the community or site, even if it's not explicitly covered by these rules.
