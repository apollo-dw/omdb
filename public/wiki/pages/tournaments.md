---
Title: Tournaments
---

**Tournaments** are organized competitions for osu! players. The most popular tournament series is [TournamentSeries1], organized officially by the osu!team. OMDB stores tournament information as a way of linking maps to tournaments, and providing context for [Descriptor55] maps.

## Rules

### Maps

- **Apply "is custom" only to maps that were made specifically for that tournament.**
- **Prioritise ranked difficulties.** Maps that eventually become ranked on a different set should be used.
- **Use original difficulties in place of edits.** This allows the original difficulty to show every tournament it was in.
- **Use reuploads if the original difficulty is missing wherever possible.** If there are multiple available, choose the one that has the greatest engagement on osu! (play count, favourites)
